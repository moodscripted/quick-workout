# Privacy policy: Quick Workout

Quick Workout stores your workout history, weights, settings and backups only on your phone.
It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your data anywhere.
Voice updates use the speech engine already on your phone. Notifications are used for workout
controls and reminders. Removing the app or clearing its storage deletes all of its data.
